05 · ABOUT SLIDESPECT

Built to become
an intelligence layer.

SlideSpect is a personal research and engineering project for combining geospatial and environmental data into a transparent landslide-risk workflow.

Slide + Spect

Slide represents landslides. Spect is inspired by inspect, observe and detect. The name reflects the purpose: observe slope conditions and turn evidence into useful insight.
