02 · RISK ANALYSIS

Risk is not
one number.

SlideSpect separates terrain susceptibility, changing triggers, historical evidence and consequences.

01

Terrain

Slope, elevation, aspect, curvature, geology and soil describe baseline susceptibility.

02

Triggers

Rainfall intensity, accumulated rainfall and soil moisture describe changing conditions.

03

History

Past landslide inventories provide labels and spatial context for supervised learning.

04

Validated model

A future calibrated model can produce probability with uncertainty and validation metrics.
